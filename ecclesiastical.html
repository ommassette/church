Formation & Tradition

Archbishop Makarios III Seminary Secondary Track

Operating as an integrated structural arm of the historical Riruta mission compound, the Patriarchal Ecclesiastical School provides essential secondary‑level theological training and preparatory formation.

Named in honor of Archbishop Makarios III, this specialized academy anchors young candidates, catechists, and future community leaders within the rigorous rhythmic life of the Orthodox Church, creating a vital foundation for advance pastoral service.

Core Disciplines

School Pillars

Theological Base

Systematic grounding in Orthodox Dogmatics, Scripture commentary, Patristics, and the canonical frameworks of the ancient Patriarchate of Alexandria.

Liturgical Arts

Comprehensive tracking in Byzantine music theory, choral arrangement, vocal performance, and precise liturgical order for structural church services.

Mission Leadership

Practical development in community mobilization, primary education oversight, parish accounting, and foundational administrative operations.
