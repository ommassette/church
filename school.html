Our Mission

Holistic Education for All

We cater specifically to underprivileged children within the Riruta‑Satellite neighborhoods of Dagoretti. Recognizing that structural community advancement requires holistic support systems, our school handles more than academic curricula.

We ensure every enrolled student receives critical daily nutrition, standard school uniforms, basic stationery materials, and primary healthcare checkups inside a secure, loving, and values‑based environment.

By removing the direct financial burdens of schooling from vulnerable families, St. Clement prevents school dropouts and secures a clear structural pathway toward secondary education and professional self‑reliance.

Beyond the Classroom

Holistic Student Care

Daily Feeding Program

Physical nutrition underpins all mental development. We serve nutritious hot breakfasts and lunches every single school day to ensure no child sits in a classroom hungry.

Uniforms & Welfare

To eliminate social stigmas and foster community dignity, the school supplies essential school uniforms, learning sportswear, shoes, and direct instructional books completely free.

Health & Well‑being

We coordinate regular healthcare monitoring, immunization reviews, and safe sanitation practices, protecting growing children from common, preventable illnesses.
