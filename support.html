How to Give

Partnership Pathways

Your generosity sustains the St. Clement mission — from the daily feeding of hundreds of schoolchildren to the training of future clergy and leaders for the Orthodox Church in Africa.

Every contribution, whether large or small, directly impacts the lives of vulnerable families in Riruta-Satellite and beyond. We invite you to partner with us in preserving Holy Tradition and serving our community.

How to Give

Partnership Pathways

Sponsor A Child

Educational & Nutritional Direct Aid

Provide direct social support to vulnerable students enrolled inside St. Clement Primary School. Sponsorship contributions directly clear structural tuition deficits, purchase full mandatory learning material uniforms, and secure regular entry into the daily hot food breakfast and lunch program grids.

Use the reference identifier "SPONSOR" followed by the child's assignment code during financial routing.

Make A Donation

Missionary & Compound Infrastructure Fund

Sustain the foundational development and maintenance operations of the extensive St. Clement missionary compound on Kabiria Road. General philanthropy directly drives the preservation of historical temple structures, purchases essential altar requirements, supports administrative staff tracking, and scales regional theological missions.

Use the reference identifier "DONATION" or specify an arm like "SEMINARY" during payment tracking.
