Our Foundation

The Mission at Riruta

An active, indigenous Parish community of the Orthodox Patriarchate of Alexandria and All Africa.

Operating under the structural governance of the Holy Metropolis of Nairobi, St. Clement keeps unchanged historical apostolic frameworks alive today through liturgical and sacramental prayer.

Beyond worship, the mission provides comprehensive structural interventions to lift families up within Riruta-Satellite neighborhoods through foundational primary education and daily nutrition programs.

Parish & School Impact

St. Clement School

Founded in September 2002. Providing exceptional, entirely 100% free foundational primary education.

Free Student Care

Ensuring essential school uniforms and consistent medical care to underprivileged community children.

Daily Feeding Program

Serving nutritious hot breakfasts and lunches every single school day to hundreds of students.

Community Growth

Active structural interventions supporting over 450+ active students and breaking dependency traps.

The Compound Centers

A Pan-African Center of Theological Learning

Our compound on Kabiria Road houses critical foundational arms of the wider Orthodox Church in Africa. This strategic alignment transforms our local parish context into a world-class center that actively shapes leaders.
